# Leopold Aschenbrenner

Former OpenAI researcher. Author of [Situational Awareness](situational-awareness.md), a comprehensive analysis of AGI development trajectories that became one of the most influential AI forecasting documents.

## Key Claims

- AGI is primarily an engineering/scaling challenge, achievable by ~2027
- The intelligence explosion (AGI → superintelligence) could happen within years
- Trillion-dollar compute clusters are coming
- AI lab security is dangerously inadequate against nation-state threats
- The US-China AI competition is the defining geopolitical contest

See sources: [Situational Awareness The Decade Ahead](../summaries/situational-awareness-the-decade-ahead.md), [I From Gpt 4 To AGI Counting The Ooms](../summaries/i-from-gpt-4-to-agi-counting-the-ooms.md)
