# Situational Awareness

A series of essays by [Leopold Aschenbrenner](leopold-aschenbrenner.md) (former OpenAI researcher) laying out a detailed scenario for AGI development through ~2030. One of the most comprehensive and influential analyses of AI trajectories.

## Structure

The series has four parts:

### I. From GPT-4 to AGI: Counting the OOMs
Argues AGI is achievable by ~2027 through continued scaling. Tracks orders of magnitude (OOMs) in compute, data, and algorithmic efficiency to project when human-level AI arrives.

See source: [I From Gpt 4 To AGI Counting The Ooms](../summaries/i-from-gpt-4-to-agi-counting-the-ooms.md)

### II. From AGI to Superintelligence: The Intelligence Explosion
Once AGI exists, AI systems can accelerate their own improvement — potentially reaching superintelligence within years. The transition may be faster than the path to AGI itself.

See source: [Ii From AGI To Superintelligence The Intelligence Explosion](../summaries/ii-from-agi-to-superintelligence-the-intelligence-explosion.md)

### III-A. Racing to the Trillion-Dollar Cluster
The industrial buildout needed: trillion-dollar compute clusters, massive power infrastructure, and the geopolitical race to build them first. Projects that individual data centers will rival small countries in power consumption.

See source: [Iiia Racing To The Trillion Dollar Cluster](../summaries/iiia-racing-to-the-trillion-dollar-cluster.md)

### III-B. Lock Down the Labs: Security for AGI
The security problem: if AGI is as powerful as predicted, the weights become the most valuable (and dangerous) artifact ever created. Current lab security is inadequate. Nation-state espionage (particularly China) is a primary threat.

See source: [Iiib Lock Down The Labs Security For AGI](../summaries/iiib-lock-down-the-labs-security-for-agi.md)

## Key Claims

- AGI is primarily an engineering/scaling challenge, not a fundamental research problem
- The US-China competition for AI supremacy is the defining geopolitical contest
- Current security practices at AI labs are dangerously insufficient
- The intelligence explosion could happen very quickly once AGI arrives
