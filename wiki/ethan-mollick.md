# Ethan Mollick

Wharton professor and one of the most prolific writers on practical AI use. Author of multiple sources in this collection, consistently focused on bridging AI capabilities with real-world application.

## Key Themes

### Practical AI Adoption
Advocates "good enough prompting" over perfectionism. Warns against the "wait calculation" trap — procrastinating adoption because better tools are always imminent.

See sources: [[sources/getting-started-with-ai-good-enough-prompting]], [[sources/the-lazy-tyranny-of-the-wait-calculation]]

### When to Use AI
Framework: 15 times to use AI (idea generation, drafting, summarization) and 5 times not to (learning, high-accuracy needs, when effort matters).

See source: [[sources/15-times-to-use-ai-and-5-not-to]]

### Model Comparison
Comprehensive guide to frontier models and their applications through different "harnesses" (chatbots, coding tools, research agents).

See source: [[sources/a-guide-to-which-ai-to-use-in-the-agentic-era]]

### Near-Term Impacts
Argues for focusing on AI's present-day impacts rather than distant AGI scenarios. Current AI already transforms work, education, and creativity.

See source: [[sources/the-present-future-ai-s-impact]]
