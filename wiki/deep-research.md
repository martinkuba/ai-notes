# Deep Research

AI tools that autonomously conduct multi-step research — browsing the web, reading documents, synthesizing findings, and producing comprehensive reports. A practical application of [[agentic-ai]] for knowledge work.

## Tools

### OpenAI Deep Research
Stunned professionals with its ability to produce research reports rivaling junior analysts. Multi-step: plans a research approach, executes searches, reads sources, synthesizes.

See source: [[sources/these-pros-were-stunned-by-openai-deep-research]]

### Perplexity Deep Research
Competitive alternative, available for free. Nearly matches OpenAI's quality.

See source: [[sources/perplexity-ai-s-deep-research-tool]]

### Google AI Co-Scientist
Research-focused agent aimed at scientific discovery — a specialized deep research agent for academia.

See source: [[sources/google-unveils-ai-co-scientist-to-supercharge-research]]

## Significance

Deep research represents the clearest near-term example of AI agents replacing knowledge worker tasks. Unlike chat, these agents work autonomously for minutes at a time, producing artifacts that previously required hours of human effort.
