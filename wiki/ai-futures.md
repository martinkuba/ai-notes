# AI Futures

Visions of how AI transforms society, organizations, and daily life — beyond the narrow question of [AGI Timelines](agi-timelines.md).

## Positive Visions

### Machines of Loving Grace
[Dario Amodei](dario-amodei.md)'s essay outlines transformative potential in biology (doubling lifespan), neuroscience, economic development, and governance. Tempered by the concept of "marginal returns to intelligence" — non-intelligence bottlenecks limit how much AI alone can achieve.

See source: [Dario Amodei Machines Of Loving Grace](../sources/dario-amodei-machines-of-loving-grace.md)

## Organizational Futures

### Fully Automated Firms
Speculation on companies run entirely by AI agents. Raises questions about ownership, accountability, legal personhood, and economic structure.

See source: [What Fully Automated Firms Will Look Like](../sources/what-fully-automated-firms-will-look-like.md)

### Business Reinvention
Agentic AI requires fundamental organizational restructuring, not just tool adoption.

See source: [Businesses Must Reinvent Themselves In The Age Of Agentic AI](../sources/businesses-must-reinvent-themselves-in-the-age-of-agentic-ai.md)

## Scenario Planning

### AI 2027
Detailed narrative scenario: "Agent-1" and "Agent-2" models, China's AI race response, algorithmic breakthroughs (neuralese, IDA), model weight theft, and cascading job displacement.

See source: [AI 2027](../sources/ai-2027.md)

### Personal Preparation
Nate Silver's framework for how individuals should prepare for an AI-transformed world.

See source: [Sbsq 17 How Should You Prepare For An AI Future](../sources/sbsq-17-how-should-you-prepare-for-an-ai-future.md)

## The Present Future

[Ethan Mollick](ethan-mollick.md) argues the most important AI impacts are happening *now*, not in some distant AGI future. Current tools already transform work, education, and creativity in meaningful ways.

See source: [The Present Future AI S Impact](../sources/the-present-future-ai-s-impact.md)
