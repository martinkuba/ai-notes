# OpenAI

Frontier AI lab. Creator of GPT, ChatGPT, the o-series reasoning models, and Deep Research. The most visible AI company in public consciousness, and the source of many of the field's defining moves — and many of its current tensions. Several of the wiki's most-cited people are OpenAI alumni: [Dario Amodei](dario-amodei.md) (left to co-found [Anthropic](anthropic.md)), [Leopold Aschenbrenner](leopold-aschenbrenner.md) (departed and wrote [Situational Awareness](situational-awareness.md)), and [Andrej Karpathy](andrej-karpathy.md).

## Products

- **GPT-5.x family** — Frontier chat models, including GPT-5.2 Thinking. See [How LLMs Work](how-llms-work.md) for the model landscape.
- **o-series / Strawberry** — [Reasoning Models](reasoning-models.md) that think before responding. The o1 launch was the field's clearest pivot from pure token prediction to structured reasoning.
- **[Deep Research](deep-research.md)** — Autonomous multi-step research agent that "stunned professionals" with junior-analyst-quality reports.
- **Swarm** — Multi-agent coordination framework. See [Agentic AI](agentic-ai.md).

## On AGI

OpenAI is the source of some of the most aggressive AGI claims:
- An OpenAI employee publicly claimed "we have already achieved AGI" with o1. See [AGI Timelines](agi-timelines.md).
- Sam Altman predicts superintelligence by ~2034.
- The fictional "ai-2027" scenario depicting Agent-1/Agent-2 model theft centers on an OpenAI-like lab. See source: [AI 2027](../summaries/ai-2027.md).

## Safety and Government Stance

- **Pentagon red lines**: Sam Altman publicly stated OpenAI shares [Anthropic](anthropic.md)'s red lines on mass surveillance and autonomous weapons — a rare cross-company alignment signal. See [AI Safety](ai-safety.md), [AI Governance](ai-governance.md).
- **Internal/external messaging tension**: OpenAI promotes utopian economic benefits while Anthropic's leadership warns of existential risks. The conflict undermines public trust. See [AI Critical Perspectives](ai-critical-perspectives.md).

## Legal Pressure

OpenAI is the defendant in the field's two highest-profile copyright cases:
- **George R.R. Martin et al. vs. OpenAI** — author lawsuit over training data. See source: [George R R Martin And Other Authors Sue Openai For](../summaries/george-r-r-martin-and-other-authors-sue-openai-for.md).
- **New York Times vs. OpenAI** — concurrent with NYT's adoption of internal AI tools. See source: [Openai Says New York Times Lawsuit Against It Is Without](../summaries/openai-says-new-york-times-lawsuit-against-it-is-without.md), [AI Governance](ai-governance.md).

## Public Backlash

OpenAI is the most visible target of an escalating AI backlash. A Molotov cocktail was allegedly thrown at Sam Altman's house. The company has responded with reputation-management efforts including media acquisitions timed to counter unflattering coverage. See [AI Critical Perspectives](ai-critical-perspectives.md).

## Pricing Pressure

Like other AI providers, OpenAI is part of the "enshittification" pattern: cheap pricing to build dependency, then per-token billing once enterprises are locked in. See source: [It Is Finally Happening](../summaries/it-is-finally-happening.md).
