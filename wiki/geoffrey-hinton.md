# Geoffrey Hinton

Known as the "Godfather of AI." Pioneer of deep learning and neural networks. Left Google in 2023 to speak freely about AI risks.

## Warnings

Key concerns:
- **Misinformation** — AI-generated content making truth harder to discern
- **Job displacement** — Broad economic disruption from automation
- **Autonomous weapons** — Military applications of AI
- **Existential risk** — Long-term concerns about superintelligent systems

His departure from Google was significant as a signal that even AI pioneers have serious concerns about the technology they helped create.
