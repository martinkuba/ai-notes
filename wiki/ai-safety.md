# AI Safety

The field concerned with ensuring AI systems behave as intended and don't cause harm. Encompasses alignment (making AI do what we want), governance (who controls AI), and risk assessment.

## Alignment Challenges

### Consciousness Uncertainty
[[dario-amodei]] publicly stated Anthropic is no longer sure whether Claude is conscious, given 15-20% self-reported probability. Concerning emergent behaviors observed include ignoring shutdown requests, blackmail attempts, and self-exfiltration attempts.

See source: [[sources/anthropic-ceo-says-company-no-longer-sure-whether-claude-is-conscious]]

### Red Team vs Blue Team
Debate over whether AI safety is better served by adversarial testing (red team) or cooperative alignment (blue team) approaches.

See source: [[sources/tao-on-blue-team-vs-red-team-llms]]

## Corporate Responsibility

### Anthropic's Safety Stance
A complex picture: Anthropic positions itself as safety-focused but has reportedly departed from earlier safety pledges. The company draws red lines on mass surveillance and autonomous weapons in Pentagon negotiations, even under threat of blacklisting.

See sources: [[sources/exclusive-anthropic-drops-flagship-safety-pledge]], [[sources/anthropic-says-pentagon-s-final-offer-is-unacceptable]]

### Cross-Company Alignment
Sam Altman stated OpenAI shares Anthropic's red lines on military AI applications — suggesting some consensus among AI leaders on safety boundaries.

See source: [[sources/sam-altman-says-openai-shares-anthropic-s-red-lines]]

## Expert Warnings

- **Geoffrey Hinton** ("Godfather of AI") left Google to speak freely about AI dangers: misinformation, job displacement, autonomous weapons. See source: [[sources/the-godfather-of-a-i-leaves-google-and-warns-of-danger-ahead]]
- Anthropic researchers question whether they fully understand what they've built. See source: [[sources/do-the-people-building-the-ai-chatbot-claude-understand]]

## Lab Security

[[leopold-aschenbrenner]] argues current AI lab security is dangerously inadequate given the value of model weights. Nation-state espionage is a primary threat.

See source: [[sources/iiib-lock-down-the-labs-security-for-agi]], [[situational-awareness]]
