# Anthropic

AI safety company. Creator of Claude. Founded by [[dario-amodei]] and Daniela Amodei (former OpenAI researchers). Positions itself as safety-focused but navigates tensions between commercial pressure and safety commitments.

## Products

- **Claude** — Foundation model family (Opus, Sonnet, Haiku)
- **[[claude-code]]** — Agentic coding tool
- **Claude Artifacts** — No-code app creation. See source: [[sources/create-ai-powered-apps-with-claude-artifacts-no-coding]]

## Safety Stance

A complex and evolving picture:
- Drew red lines against mass surveillance and autonomous weapons in Pentagon negotiations, even under threat of blacklisting. See source: [[sources/anthropic-says-pentagon-s-final-offer-is-unacceptable]]
