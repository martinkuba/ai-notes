# AI Governance

The political, regulatory, and institutional frameworks around AI development and deployment.

## US Government

### Manhattan Project for AI
Trump administration allies drafted executive orders to launch "Manhattan Projects" for defense AI — massive government-funded AI R&D programs for national security applications.

See source: [Trump Allies Draft AI Order To Launch Manhattan Projects](../sources/trump-allies-draft-ai-order-to-launch-manhattan-projects.md)

### Military AI Red Lines
Both [Anthropic](anthropic.md) and OpenAI drew lines against mass surveillance and autonomous weapons when negotiating with the Pentagon. Anthropic faced threats of blacklisting and Defense Production Act invocation for refusing to compromise.

See sources: [Anthropic Says Pentagon S Final Offer Is Unacceptable](../sources/anthropic-says-pentagon-s-final-offer-is-unacceptable.md), [Sam Altman Says Openai Shares Anthropic S Red Lines](../sources/sam-altman-says-openai-shares-anthropic-s-red-lines.md)

## Democratic Governance

Questions about whether AI companies should need public permission for decisions that affect society at scale. Current development is driven almost entirely by corporate priorities.

See source: [Democratic AI Should Companies Like Openai And Anthropic](../sources/democratic-ai-should-companies-like-openai-and-anthropic.md)
