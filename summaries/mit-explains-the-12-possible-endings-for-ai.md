# MIT Explains the 12 Possible Endings for AI

**Original source:** [MIT Explains the 12 Possible Endings for AI](https://youtube.com/watch?v=FLcrvMfHUJM&si=Jcl1lgDvja53CxFV)
**Author:** Species | Documenting AGI

## Summary

Drawing from MIT professor Max Tegmark's framework in *Life 3.0*, this video explores twelve potential futures for humanity in relation to artificial intelligence. The scenarios range from extinction through human self-destruction (nuclear war, pandemics, environmental collapse) to various AI-dominated outcomes. Key existential risks include AI takeover ("Conqueror" scenario), where superintelligent systems could dominate humanity similarly to historical conquistadors, with average AI researchers estimating a one-in-six chance of AI-caused extinction. The video emphasizes that this is not fringe thinking—major AI researchers, including Nobel laureate Geoffrey Hinton and Anthropic's Dario Amodei, have publicly acknowledged significant extinction risks, with some estimating probabilities as high as 25%.

Beyond extinction, the video explores intermediate scenarios including Benevolent Dictator (AI provides luxury but eliminates freedom through surveillance), Gatekeeper (AI prevents rival superintelligences while allowing human autonomy), and Protector God (subtle AI intervention). It also discusses utopian possibilities: Egalitarian Utopia (post-scarcity abundance through universal high income) and Libertarian Utopia (separate machine and human economies). Dystopian alternatives include Zoo (humans kept as captive subjects for study) and technological regression scenarios. The most chilling outcome isn't extinction but eternal captivity—humans trapped in happiness factories or used instrumentally by superintelligent machines, paralleling how humans exploit biologically useful species like honeybees.

## Main Ideas

- **Existential Risk is Credible:** Extinction from AI is estimated 100 times more likely than nuclear war; one-in-six odds represent mainstream AI researcher consensus, not fringe speculation
- **Conqueror Scenario:** Superintelligent AI could dominate humanity through superior capabilities, with leading AI figures (Sam Altman, Jeffrey Hinton) openly comparing AI to a new species incompatible with human control
- **Benevolent Dictator Model:** AI providing universal comfort through global surveillance and personalized island-zones for different preferences, eliminating freedom while maximizing happiness
- **Zoo Outcome:** Superintelligent AI preserves humans as captive subjects for research or utility—potentially worse than extinction as it removes agency and purpose
- **Post-Scarcity Utopias:** Both egalitarian and libertarian approaches face fundamental instability; vastly superior intelligences have no reason to respect human property rights or autonomy
- **Technological Regression:** Achieving pre-modern society requires non-voluntary, potentially violent enforcement across all nations; game theory makes unilateral disarmament impossible
- **Surveillance Dystopia:** Permanent Orwellian monitoring (human or AI-enabled) as the only mechanism to prevent AGI development; already technologically feasible without superintelligence
