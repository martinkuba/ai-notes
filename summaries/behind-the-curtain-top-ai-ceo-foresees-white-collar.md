# Behind the Curtain: Top AI CEO foresees white-collar bloodbath

**Original source:** [Behind the Curtain: Top AI CEO foresees white-collar bloodbath](https://www.axios.com/2025/05/28/ai-jobs-white-collar-unemployment-anthropic)
**Author:** Jim VandeHei

## Summary

Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei delivered a blunt warning that AI could wipe out half of all entry-level white-collar jobs and spike unemployment to 10-20% within one to five years. He argues AI companies and government need to stop "sugar-coating" what's coming: a possible mass elimination of jobs across technology, finance, law, consulting, and other white-collar professions. Amodei spoke out publicly while acknowledging the irony of building the very technology he predicts could reorder society overnight—on the same day he was onstage touting Claude 4's capabilities. He framed his warning as a duty: "We, as the producers of this technology, have a duty and an obligation to be honest about what is coming."

The article details how the displacement scenario could unfold: AI models keep improving beyond human performance, the government says little, most Americans remain unaware, and then businesses see the savings of replacing humans en masse—almost overnight. Meta's Zuckerberg, Microsoft, CrowdStrike, and Walmart are already making moves. Every CEO the authors spoke with is working to figure out when AI agents can displace human workers at scale. Anthropic's own research shows AI is currently used mainly for augmentation, but Amodei predicts a rapid tip toward full automation within "a couple of years or less."

Amodei proposed several mitigations including increased public awareness, helping workers understand AI augmentation, better-informed policymakers, and a "token tax" where ~3% of AI company revenue would be redistributed. He emphasized that "you can't just step in front of the train and stop it" but believes steering the technology 10 degrees differently is still possible—if action is taken now.

## Main Ideas

- Amodei predicts AI could eliminate half of entry-level white-collar jobs and drive 10-20% unemployment within 1-5 years
- The shift from AI augmentation to AI automation could happen in "a couple of years or less"
- Every CEO interviewed is actively working to determine when AI can replace human workers at scale
- Amodei proposed a "token tax" of ~3% on AI company revenue to fund redistribution
- The Anthropic Economic Index and Economic Advisory Council were created to provide transparency about workforce changes
- Amodei acknowledges the contradiction of warning about risks while actively building the technology

## Key Quotes

- "Most of them are unaware that this is about to happen. It sounds crazy, and people just don't believe it." — Dario Amodei
- "Cancer is cured, the economy grows at 10% a year, the budget is balanced — and 20% of people don't have jobs." — Amodei on a possible AI future
- "You can't just step in front of the train and stop it. The only move that's going to work is steering the train." — Dario Amodei
