# Unlike X, Bluesky says it won't train AI on your posts

**Original source:** [Unlike X, Bluesky says it won't train AI on your posts](https://techcrunch.com/2024/11/15/unlike-x-bluesky-says-it-wont-train-ai-on-your-posts/)
**Author:** Aisha Malik

## Summary

As Bluesky experienced a surge of over 1 million new signups in 24 hours amid a mass exodus from X (formerly Twitter) following the US presidential election, the social network announced it has "no intention" of using user content to train generative AI tools. This announcement was strategically timed on the same day X's new terms of service took effect, which explicitly grant the platform license to use public posts for AI training, including for third-party use.

Bluesky acknowledged that it uses AI internally for content moderation and its "Discover" algorithmic feed but emphasized that "none of these are Gen AI systems trained on user content." The policy directly addresses concerns from artists and creators who have made Bluesky their home. Meanwhile, X's updated privacy policy allows the company to "analyze text and other information" for "training of our machine learning and artificial intelligence models, whether generative or another type." The contrast highlights how AI training data policies are becoming a key differentiator in social media platform competition, particularly in attracting creative communities.

## Main Ideas

- Bluesky explicitly committed to not using user content for generative AI training
- The announcement was timed to coincide with X's new terms of service allowing AI training on user posts
- Bluesky saw over 1 million new signups in 24 hours as users migrated from X
- AI training data policies are becoming a competitive differentiator for social platforms
- Bluesky uses AI internally for moderation and discovery but not for generative AI training
- Despite growth, Meta's Threads (275M MAU) remains the largest X competitor
