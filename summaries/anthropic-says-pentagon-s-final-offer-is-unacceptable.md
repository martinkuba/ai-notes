# Anthropic says Pentagon's "final offer" is unacceptable

**Original source:** [Anthropic says Pentagon's "final offer" is unacceptable](https://www.axios.com/2026/02/26/anthropic-rejects-pentagon-ai-terms)
**Author:** Maria Curi, Dave Lawler

## Summary

Anthropic and the Pentagon are locked in a high-stakes standoff over the conditions under which the Department of War can use Anthropic's AI model Claude in classified settings. CEO Dario Amodei stated that "virtually no progress" had been made in negotiations, with a Friday deadline fast approaching. Anthropic's core objection is that the Pentagon's proposed contract language, while framed as a compromise, included legal loopholes that would allow safeguards against mass surveillance of Americans and fully autonomous weapons to be "disregarded at will."

The Pentagon has threatened two contradictory consequences: blacklisting Anthropic as a supply chain risk (asking defense contractors like Boeing and Lockheed Martin to assess their exposure) or invoking the Defense Production Act to compel Anthropic to provide Claude without restrictions. Amodei pointed out the inherent contradiction—one labels the company a security risk while the other labels Claude as essential to national security. Despite these pressures, Anthropic refused to back down, with Amodei stating the company "cannot in good conscience accede to their request."

The dispute highlights a broader policy requiring AI models in classified settings to be offered for "all lawful purposes." While Anthropic has been the only model used in classified settings to date, competitors like xAI have already signed contracts under the Pentagon's terms, and negotiations to bring OpenAI and Google into the classified space are accelerating.

## Main Ideas

- Anthropic rejected the Pentagon's "final offer" over AI safeguards, specifically opposing use of Claude for mass surveillance or autonomous weapons
- The Pentagon threatened both blacklisting Anthropic and invoking the Defense Production Act—contradictory pressures Amodei publicly highlighted
- Contract language presented as compromise contained legal loopholes that would effectively nullify the proposed safeguards
- Competitors like xAI have already accepted the Pentagon's "all lawful purposes" standard for classified work
- Anthropic remained willing to negotiate but offered to enable a smooth transition to another AI provider if talks collapsed
- Pentagon official Emil Michael denounced Amodei as a "liar" with a "God complex," escalating the confrontation

## Key Quotes

- "The contract language we received overnight from the Department of War made virtually no progress on preventing Claude's use for mass surveillance of Americans or in fully autonomous weapons." — Anthropic statement
- "These threats are inherently contradictory: one labels us a security risk; the other labels Claude as essential to national security." — Dario Amodei
