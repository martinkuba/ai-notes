# When people think software engineers, they tend to think jobs...

**Original source:** [When people think software engineers, they tend to think jobs...](https://x.com/levie/status/2045616837787070695/?s=12&rw_tt_thread=True)
**Author:** Aaron Levie

## Summary

Aaron Levie argues that the emergence of AI agents will fundamentally transform the software engineering industry beyond its current association with tech company product development. Rather than representing a threat to engineering jobs, AI agents will democratize software development across every sector of the economy—from biopharma and industrial manufacturing to banking and retail—making technical automation feasible and affordable for organizations that previously couldn't justify the cost or complexity.

The shift will manifest in two major ways: first, non-tech companies will massively expand their internal software engineering needs to build backend systems, automate workflows, and create new digital experiences; second, the nature of software engineering work itself will evolve. Engineers will focus less on building consumer-facing applications and more on system design, agent orchestration, workflow automation, business process redesign, and output validation. Levie cites real-world evidence like Eli Lilly's "Lab Automation Software Engineer" position as early examples of this emerging job category, suggesting that similar technical roles will proliferate across all industries as companies learn to leverage AI agents to transform their operations.

## Main Ideas

- **Industry-wide transformation**: AI agents will shift software engineering demand from tech companies to every sector of the economy (biopharma, finance, retail, consulting, industrial)
- **Expanded engineering roles**: Traditional companies will hire engineers not to build consumer apps but to implement automation, manage agents, redesign business processes, and maintain complex systems
- **Economic democratization**: AI makes previously infeasible or unaffordable technical projects accessible to smaller teams (5 people handling work that once required 50)
- **New job category emergence**: Specialized roles like "Lab Automation Software Engineer" represent a wave of domain-specific engineering positions across industries
- **Reframing the engineering future**: Rather than replacement, this represents a massive expansion of engineering demand with different focus areas than traditional tech industry roles
