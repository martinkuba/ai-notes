# These are the top 10 most popular AI tools of 2023, and how to use them to make more money

**Original source:** [These are the top 10 most popular AI tools of 2023, and how to use them to make more money](https://www.cnbc.com/2023/12/24/the-top-10-ai-tools-of-2023-and-how-to-use-them-to-make-more-money.html)
**Author:** Ashton Jackson

## Summary

This CNBC article ranks the top 10 most popular AI tools of 2023 based on a Writerbuddy.ai analysis of over 3,000 AI tools using SEMrush data. ChatGPT dominated overwhelmingly with 14.6 billion visits between September 2022 and August 2023, far outpacing the second-place Character.ai at 3.8 billion visits. The top 50 AI tools collectively attracted over 24 billion visits, with the majority of traffic coming from male users.

The full top 10 list includes ChatGPT, Character.ai, Quillbot, Midjourney, Hugging Face, Bard (Google), NovelAI, Capcut, Janitor AI, and Civitai. The tools span categories including AI chatbots, writing assistants, image generators, video generators, and data science platforms. Chatbots dominated the rankings, occupying four of the top ten spots. Despite negative press around Sam Altman's leadership shakeup, ChatGPT's popularity remained unshaken.

The article also offers practical advice on using AI tools for income generation. Susan Gonzales, CEO of AIandYou, suggests that freelancers and small business owners can use AI to improve inventory management, analyze customer behavior, target marketing more effectively, and identify new revenue opportunities. She also highlights opportunities in AI tutoring and training as emerging income streams.

## Main Ideas

- ChatGPT dominated AI tool usage in 2023 with 14.6 billion visits, far ahead of competitors
- AI chatbots were the most popular category, holding 4 of the top 10 spots
- The top 50 AI tools collectively attracted over 24 billion visits
- Character.ai and NovelAI suggest significant demand for AI as virtual companions
- Small businesses can leverage AI for marketing, inventory management, and competitive intelligence
- AI tutoring and training represent emerging income opportunities
