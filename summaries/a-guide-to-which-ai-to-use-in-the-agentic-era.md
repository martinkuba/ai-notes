# A Guide to Which AI to Use in the Agentic Era

**Original source:** [A Guide to Which AI to Use in the Agentic Era](https://www.oneusefulthing.org/p/a-guide-to-which-ai-to-use-in-the)
**Author:** Ethan Mollick

## Summary

Ethan Mollick argues that "using AI" has fundamentally shifted from chatbot conversations to agentic workflows, and users now need to think about three distinct layers: Models, Apps, and Harnesses. Models are the underlying AI brains (GPT-5.2/5.3, Claude Opus 4.6, Gemini 3 Pro), and while benchmarks compare them, the differences between frontier models have narrowed considerably. Apps are the products people interact with, such as chatgpt.com, claude.ai, or gemini.google.com, along with specialized tools like coding agents. Harnesses are the systems that let models use tools, take actions, and complete multi-step tasks autonomously -- and they are increasingly what differentiates the user experience.

Mollick walks through the current landscape in detail. For chatbot interfaces, he notes that ChatGPT and Claude have stronger harnesses than Gemini, producing working spreadsheets and PowerPoints with citations, while Gemini's website lags despite its model being competitive. He stresses that the single most important thing users can do is manually select the most capable model rather than relying on "auto" mode, which often defaults to weaker variants. Beyond chatbots, he highlights coding agents (Claude Code, OpenAI Codex, Google Antigravity), Claude's Excel and PowerPoint integrations, Claude Cowork (a desktop agent for non-technical work), NotebookLM for research synthesis, and OpenClaw as an open-source personal assistant with significant security caveats.

The practical advice is straightforward: pick one of the three ecosystems, pay the $20/month, select the advanced model, and start using AI for real work. For experienced users, he recommends trying specialized apps like NotebookLM, Claude Code, and Claude Cowork to experience the shift from chatbot to agent firsthand.

## Main Ideas

- The AI landscape now requires understanding three layers: Models (the AI brain), Apps (the interface), and Harnesses (tool access and autonomy).
- The top three frontier models (GPT-5.2, Claude Opus 4.6, Gemini 3 Pro) are close in capability; the harness and app matter more than the model for most users.
- Users must manually select advanced models rather than relying on default "auto" mode, which often routes to weaker variants.
- Claude Code, OpenAI Codex, and Google Antigravity represent the most powerful harnesses, enabling autonomous multi-step coding and project execution.
- Claude Cowork extends agentic capability to non-technical knowledge work, running on the desktop with file and browser access in a sandboxed VM.
- The shift from chatbot to agent is the most important change in how people use AI since ChatGPT launched.

## Key Quotes

- "An AI that does things is fundamentally more useful than an AI that says things, and learning to use it that way is worth your time."
- "The single most important thing you can do is pick the right model, which the AI companies do not make easy."
- "You aren't prompting, you are managing."
