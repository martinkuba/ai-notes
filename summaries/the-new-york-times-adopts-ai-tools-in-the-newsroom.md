# The New York Times adopts AI tools in the newsroom

**Original source:** [The New York Times adopts AI tools in the newsroom](https://www.theverge.com/news/613989/new-york-times-internal-ai-tools-echo)
**Author:** Jess Weatherbed

## Summary

The New York Times has rolled out approved AI tools across its newsroom, including a new internal tool called Echo for summarizing articles, briefings, and company activity. Staff received new editorial guidelines and mandatory training detailing permitted AI uses: suggesting edits and revisions, generating summaries, writing promotional copy for social media and SEO headlines, developing news quizzes and quote cards, and even suggesting questions reporters should ask during interviews.

However, the Times drew clear restrictions: AI should not be used to draft or significantly alter articles, circumvent paywalls, input third-party copyrighted materials, or publish AI-generated images or videos without explicit labeling. The outlet reaffirmed its commitment that journalism will always be "reported, written and edited by expert journalists," with AI used to assist parts of the process under journalist accountability. Beyond Echo, approved tools include GitHub Copilot, Google Vertex AI, NotebookLM, the NYT's ChatExplorer, OpenAI's non-ChatGPT API, and some Amazon AI products. The rollout is notable given that the Times is simultaneously suing OpenAI and Microsoft for allegedly training ChatGPT on Times content without permission, creating a tension between opposing external AI use of their content while embracing internal AI tools.

## Main Ideas

- The NYT introduced Echo, an internal AI tool for summarizing articles and company activity, alongside approved third-party tools
- Permitted uses include editing suggestions, summaries, social media copy, SEO headlines, quiz development, and interview question generation
- Key restrictions: AI cannot draft articles, alter article content significantly, or publish unlabeled AI-generated media
- The Times maintains its commitment to human-led journalism with AI as a process assistant, not a content creator
- The rollout creates a notable tension with the NYT's ongoing copyright lawsuit against OpenAI and Microsoft
- Mandatory AI training was rolled out to both product and editorial staff
