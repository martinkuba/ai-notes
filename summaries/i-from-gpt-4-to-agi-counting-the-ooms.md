# I. From GPT-4 to AGI: Counting the OOMs

**Original source:** [I. From GPT-4 to AGI: Counting the OOMs](https://situational-awareness.ai/from-gpt-4-to-agi/)
**Author:** SITUATIONAL AWARENESS - The Decade Ahead

## Summary

This essay from Leopold Aschenbrenner's "Situational Awareness" series argues that AGI by 2027 is strikingly plausible by simply extrapolating existing trends. The core method is "counting the OOMs" (orders of magnitude): tracing the growth in three categories of improvement -- physical compute, algorithmic efficiencies, and "unhobbling" gains -- over the four years from GPT-2 to GPT-4, then projecting what we should expect in the four years after GPT-4 through the end of 2027.

The piece traces how GPT-2 (2019, roughly preschooler-level) progressed to GPT-4 (2023, roughly smart high-schooler-level) through approximately 3,000x-10,000x more raw compute and 1-2 OOMs of algorithmic efficiency gains. "Unhobbling" techniques like RLHF, chain-of-thought prompting, scaffolding, tool use, and extended context windows unlocked massive latent capabilities from base models. For the next four years, the author projects another ~100,000x effective compute scaleup: roughly 2 additional OOMs of physical compute (driven by trillion-dollar cluster investments), ~2 OOMs of algorithmic efficiencies, plus continued unhobbling gains that should transform models from chatbots into agent-like "drop-in remote workers."

The essay emphasizes that deep learning has consistently defied skeptics, rapidly saturating benchmarks that experts predicted would take years. The author argues that the resulting AI systems would be able to automate cognitive jobs that could be done remotely, and critically, if they can automate AI research itself, this would trigger intense feedback loops leading from AGI to superintelligence. The error bars are acknowledged as large -- data walls, insufficient unhobbling, or scaling breakdowns could slow things down -- but the central claim is that mere trend extrapolation of straight lines makes AGI by 2027 a serious possibility.

## Main Ideas

- AGI by 2027 is plausible based on extrapolating consistent trends in compute scaling, algorithmic efficiency, and unhobbling gains.
- GPT-2 to GPT-4 represented roughly a 4.5-6 OOM effective compute scaleup plus major unhobbling gains, taking models from preschooler to smart high-schooler level.
- Three drivers of progress: physical compute (~0.5 OOMs/year from investment, not Moore's Law), algorithmic efficiencies (~0.5 OOMs/year), and unhobbling (RLHF, chain-of-thought, scaffolding, tools, context length).
- Another ~100,000x effective compute scaleup is projected by end of 2027, which should produce another qualitative jump comparable to GPT-2-to-GPT-4.
- Unhobbling is critical: the future is not just a smarter chatbot but agent-like systems that can work independently as "drop-in remote workers."
- If AI systems can automate AI research, this triggers feedback loops that could compress a decade of algorithmic progress into a year.
- Deep learning has consistently surpassed expert predictions and saturated benchmarks far faster than anticipated.
