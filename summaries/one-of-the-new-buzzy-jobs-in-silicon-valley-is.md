# One of the new, buzzy jobs in Silicon Valley is...

**Original source:** [One of the new, buzzy jobs in Silicon Valley is...](https://x.com/AndrewYNg/status/2061477558693384395/?rw_tt_thread=True)
**Author:** Andrew Ng

## Summary

Forward Deployed Engineers (FDEs) have become a buzzy new role in Silicon Valley, with companies like OpenAI and Anthropic building dedicated teams to place FDEs within client organizations. The FDE role—pioneered by Palantir about two decades ago—involves embedding engineers directly in client organizations to help customize AI solutions and build agentic workflows tailored to specific business needs. Beyond technical skills, FDEs require strong communication and business acumen to understand client requirements, prioritize projects, explain complex technology, and manage expectations.

However, Andrew Ng argues that AI Engineer jobs will significantly outnumber FDE positions going forward. While companies might accept a small number of embedded FDEs, they will hire far more AI Engineers to work internally on their own projects. A key concern is vendor lock-in: FDEs tend to tightly integrate a specific vendor's products into company processes, reducing optionality at a time when the AI landscape is still highly uncertain. Companies increasingly value the flexibility to switch vendors in the future.

The demand for AI Engineers—generalists who can build software applications with AI components (LLM prompting, agentic frameworks, evaluations) and effectively use AI coding agents—is currently very high. As the field matures over the next decade, Ng expects the generic AI Engineer role to specialize, similar to how software engineering fragmented into frontend, backend, mobile, data engineering, DevOps, and other disciplines. New specialized roles such as LLMOps Engineers, Evals Engineers, and AI Data Engineers will likely emerge.

## Main Ideas

- Forward Deployed Engineers (FDEs) embed within client organizations to customize AI solutions and build agentic workflows
- FDEs require technical skills plus communication and business expertise
- AI Engineer jobs will far exceed FDE positions because companies prefer internal engineering teams
- Vendor lock-in concerns make clients wary of FDEs who tightly bind them to specific AI vendors
- Strong current demand for generalist AI Engineers skilled in LLM components, agentic frameworks, and AI coding agents
- The AI Engineer role will likely specialize over time into distinct disciplines (LLMOps, Evals, Data Engineering, etc.)
- Job market expansion in AI will be substantial, contradicting "jobpocalypse" narratives
