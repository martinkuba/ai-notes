# The more enterprises I talk to about AI agent transformation...

**Original source:** [The more enterprises I talk to about AI agent transformation...](https://x.com/levie/status/2043883641366032638/?rw_tt_thread=True)
**Author:** Aaron Levie

## Summary

Aaron Levie identifies an emerging organizational role that will become critical as enterprises adopt AI agents: the agent deployer and manager. This person acts as a technical-business hybrid who identifies high-leverage workflows where AI agents can deliver transformative value—enabling tasks to be executed 100x faster or performed 100x more frequently. Examples include automating lead qualification and customer signal enrichment, streamlining contract review processes, and creating enterprise knowledge bases.

The role requires deep process mapping expertise combined with technical fluency in connecting business systems, managing data flows (both structured and unstructured), and configuring agent context and human-agent interaction points. These professionals must understand where value can be unlocked through automation, design future-state workflows, and manage ongoing operations including evaluations, monitoring, and KPI tracking. They need comfort with emerging AI tooling like skills, MCP (Model Context Protocol), and CLI tools, with organizational trust to autonomously connect systems.

The position may emerge through internal repositioning or new hiring, likely distributed across teams rather than centralized, though potentially with oversight from IT or a central AI function. This represents a significant career opportunity for technically-minded AI-focused talent and engineers seeking future-proof roles.

## Main Ideas

- A new organizational role—agent deployer and manager—will emerge as a critical position across most enterprises adopting AI
- This person identifies workflows where AI agents can drive 100x improvements in speed or scale of execution
- The role combines technical skills (data mapping, system integration, API/CLI/MCP expertise) with operational and business acumen
- Key responsibilities include workflow design, agent context configuration, human-in-the-loop interface design, and ongoing performance management
- The position may be decentralized (one or more per team) rather than centralized, though with potential central governance oversight
- This represents strong career opportunities for next-generation technical hires and engineers repositioning for the AI era
