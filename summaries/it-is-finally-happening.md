# It Is Finally Happening

**Original source:** [It Is Finally Happening](https://youtube.com/watch?v=c1cBGW_zoyQ&si=0nSIgxgUnmGdjJe1)
**Author:** Kyle Cook from Web Dev Simplified

## Summary

AI companies are beginning to show the "enshittification" pattern typical of VC-funded tech startups, as they can no longer sustain the unsustainable pricing models that initially attracted users. After years of offering generous token allowances at artificially low prices sustained by venture capital, companies like GitHub Copilot and Anthropic are now implementing drastic changes: restricting usage limits, removing features from lower-tier plans, suspending new signups, and shifting to per-token pricing models. This represents a classic tech industry cycle where companies use cheap or free offerings to build massive user bases, then dramatically increase prices and reduce features once customer dependency is established.

The pattern mirrors historical examples like Netflix, Uber, and DoorDash, which all transitioned from loss-leading bargains to significantly higher prices with reduced value. However, AI tools present a particularly concerning case because many developers and enterprises may become so dependent on AI for core business functions that they have no viable alternative but to pay whatever prices are demanded. The financial pressure is immense—companies are currently losing money on power users who hit usage limits, making current pricing unsustainable. Cook predicts this pricing correction will be even more severe for enterprise customers managing hundreds or thousands of users, potentially causing significant economic disruption across AI-dependent industries.

## Main Ideas

- AI companies have been operating at massive losses by offering unlimited or extremely generous token usage at low monthly prices, subsidized by venture capital
- GitHub Copilot and Anthropic recently implemented drastic plan changes: suspended signups, stricter usage limits, feature removal, and significantly higher prices for premium tiers
- This follows the classic "enshittification" pattern seen in Netflix, Uber, and DoorDash: low prices to gain market dominance, then price increases and feature reductions once users are locked in
- AI represents an extreme case because businesses and developers who become dependent on AI for core functions may lack viable alternatives
- Enterprise customers are being transitioned from per-user pricing to per-token billing, dramatically increasing costs for large organizations
- The economic impact will be severe, especially for industries heavily dependent on AI like software development, potentially causing major business disruption
