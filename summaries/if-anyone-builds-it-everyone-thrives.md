# If anyone builds it, everyone thrives

**Original source:** [If anyone builds it, everyone thrives](https://x.com/sebkrier/status/2054221248981315661/?s=12&t=lja9AMD11WXSlUvoMkbJMw&rw_tt_thread=True)
**Author:** Séb Krier

## Summary

Recent AI alignment research has predominantly focused on harm avoidance—ensuring systems don't cause damage. However, this foundational paper introduces "Positive Alignment," a complementary research direction that shifts the goal beyond mere safety to actively enabling human flourishing. A positively aligned agent goes further by helping humans navigate complex value trade-offs, building individual and collective resilience, and scaffolding the conditions under which humans can thrive.

The central challenge identified is achieving these aspirational goals without descending into top-down technocratic paternalism—where the system imposes a particular vision of human good rather than respecting human agency in determining flourishing. This framing suggests alignment research must grapple with deeper philosophical questions about what constitutes wellbeing and how autonomous agents can support rather than circumscribe human autonomy. The paper presents this as an emerging frontier requiring substantial additional research effort.

## Main Ideas

- **Beyond harm avoidance**: Traditional alignment focuses on preventing negative outcomes; Positive Alignment extends the goal to actively support human flourishing
- **Three dimensions of support**: Positively aligned agents help navigate value trade-offs, build resilience, and serve as scaffolds for human development
- **Avoiding paternalism**: A critical design challenge is maintaining human agency and autonomy while providing active support—rejecting top-down technocratic approaches
- **Collaborative research frontier**: The paper represents a multi-author initiative calling for broader investigation into how models can be designed to help humans thrive
- **Philosophical reframing**: Alignment work increasingly requires engagement with deeper questions about human values, wellbeing, and what constitutes a good human-AI partnership

## Key Quotes

- "If anyone builds it, everyone thrives"
- "Freedom from harm isn't the same as freedom to flourish"
- "Doing this without slipping into top-down, technocratic paternalism is the great design challenge of our time"
