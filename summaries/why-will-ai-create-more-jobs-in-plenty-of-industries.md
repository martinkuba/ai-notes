# Why will AI create more jobs in plenty of industries?

**Original source:** [Why will AI create more jobs in plenty of industries?](https://x.com/levie/status/2044621545348481285/?s=12&rw_tt_thread=True)
**Author:** Aaron Levie

## Summary

Aaron Levie argues that AI will create job growth across many industries despite automating specific tasks. The mechanism works through cascading bottlenecks: when AI accelerates output in one area, it shifts constraints downstream to processes still requiring human involvement. For example, AI legal agents handling more inquiries from customers creates increased demand for human lawyers. This pattern repeats across sectors—automating patient referrals creates bottlenecks in doctor availability; AI customer outreach generates more sales conversations; increased security automation requires more security researchers. Beyond these direct effects, AI-driven acceleration of business formation, patent filings, and scientific research ultimately routes more work through regulatory and specialized human functions. While some areas will see true efficiency losses and reduced spending, the competitive dynamics of markets prevent sustained job losses. As competitors leverage AI to improve products, reach more customers, and optimize operations, businesses must match these capabilities or risk failure, creating persistent demand for human labor across the value chain.

## Main Ideas

- AI creates job growth by automating one step, which reveals downstream bottlenecks still requiring human expertise
- Legal services exemplify this pattern: AI agents handling inquiries increases demand for human lawyers to resolve complex cases
- AI-driven business acceleration (new companies, patents, research) generates downstream work in regulatory and professional services
- Automation in security, healthcare, and sales creates new human-intensive bottlenecks rather than eliminating jobs
- Competitive pressure forces companies to invest in complementary human capabilities to match competitors' AI-enhanced offerings
- Static efficiency gains are limited; dynamic markets continuously create new work as competitive advantages are matched

## Key Quotes

"It's because we're going to use AI to accelerate output in one area, and then eventually you run into a new bottleneck somewhere else in the process that still requires humans."

"Your competitor will use AI to build a better product, go out and meet with even more customers, deliver a better service, run better ad campaigns, and you eventually have to match them or die."
