# Trump allies draft AI order to launch 'Manhattan Projects' for defense

**Original source:** [Trump allies draft AI order to launch 'Manhattan Projects' for defense - The Washington Post](https://www.washingtonpost.com/technology/2024/07/16/trump-ai-executive-order-regulations-military/)
**Author:** Cat Zakrzewski

## Summary

This Washington Post article reports that allies of former President Donald Trump drafted a sweeping AI executive order that would launch a series of "Manhattan Projects" to develop military AI technology and immediately review "unnecessary and burdensome regulations." The plan, titled "Make America First in AI," signals how a potential second Trump administration would pursue AI policies favorable to Silicon Valley investors and companies, rolling back regulatory oversight while channeling resources into defense applications.

The draft executive order represents a significant departure from the regulatory approach taken by the Biden administration, favoring rapid military AI development and deregulation. Silicon Valley investors were described as flocking to support the former president, likely attracted by the prospect of reduced regulatory barriers and increased defense spending on AI technology.

## Main Ideas

- Trump allies drafted an AI executive order called "Make America First in AI"
- The plan would launch "Manhattan Projects" for military AI technology development
- It calls for immediate review and rollback of "unnecessary and burdensome" AI regulations
- The approach favors Silicon Valley investors and companies
- The policy represents a shift toward deregulation and military AI investment
