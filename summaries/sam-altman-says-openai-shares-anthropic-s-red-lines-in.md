# Sam Altman says OpenAI shares Anthropic's red lines in Pentagon fight

**Original source:** [Sam Altman says OpenAI shares Anthropic's red lines in Pentagon fight](https://www.axios.com/2026/02/27/altman-openai-anthropic-pentagon)
**Author:** Maria Curi, Dave Lawler

## Summary

In a staff memo obtained by Axios, OpenAI CEO Sam Altman declared that OpenAI shares Anthropic's "red lines" regarding military AI use: no AI for mass surveillance or autonomous lethal weapons, with humans remaining in the loop for high-stakes automated decisions. This came after Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei rejected the Pentagon's demand that AI companies agree their models could be used for "all lawful purposes" without company-specific guardrails, leading the Pentagon to consider declaring Anthropic a "supply chain risk."

Altman positioned his stance as an industry-wide issue rather than just an Anthropic-Pentagon dispute, while still expressing interest in striking a deal for ChatGPT deployment in classified military environments. OpenAI's proposed safeguards include continuous security monitoring, security-cleared researchers tracking technology use, and confining models to cloud environments rather than edge deployments like autonomous weapons. The Pentagon's primary objection is having private companies dictate how the government uses AI for national security, particularly during technological competition with China. Meanwhile, employees from OpenAI and Google signed a solidarity letter supporting Anthropic's position, and Elon Musk's xAI (Grok) agreed to the Pentagon's unrestricted terms, though it isn't seen as a viable Claude replacement.

## Main Ideas

- Leading AI companies (OpenAI, Anthropic) are drawing shared red lines against mass surveillance and autonomous lethal weapons in military AI use
- The Pentagon wants unrestricted "all lawful purposes" access to AI models, which conflicts with company-specific ethical guardrails
- OpenAI seeks a middle path: classified military deployment with specific exclusions for surveillance and autonomous offensive weapons
- Cross-company employee solidarity emerged, with OpenAI and Google workers signing letters supporting Anthropic's stance
- The dispute highlights a fundamental tension between private company ethics and government sovereignty over national security tools
- xAI (Grok) accepted the Pentagon's unrestricted terms, but isn't considered a viable alternative to Claude for sensitive military work
