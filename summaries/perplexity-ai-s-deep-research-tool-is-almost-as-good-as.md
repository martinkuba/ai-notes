# Perplexity AI's Deep Research Tool Is Almost as Good as OpenAI's, and It's Free

**Original source:** [Perplexity AI's Deep Research Tool Is Almost as Good as OpenAI's, and It's Free](https://lifehacker.com/tech/perplexity-ai-deep-research-almost-as-good-as-openai-and-free)
**Author:** Khamosh Pathak

## Summary

Perplexity AI launched a Deep Research feature that performs dozens of related searches, processes hundreds of resources, and uses a reasoning model to produce comprehensive, white-paper-style reports — and notably, it is available to free users (limited to five queries per day). This makes Perplexity the first major AI provider to offer deep research capabilities at no cost, following DeepSeek's lead in democratizing access to reasoning models. By contrast, OpenAI's Deep Research feature is restricted to paying users and heavily limited below the $200/month ChatGPT Pro tier.

The two products differ significantly in depth and speed. OpenAI's version takes up to 20 minutes per query, asks follow-up questions, and produces very long reports using its o3 reasoning model. Perplexity's version returns results in 2-4 minutes, relying more on web search and data collation than deep AI interpretation — making it a "lite" version of the concept. Despite this trade-off, Perplexity performed respectably on Humanity's Last Exam benchmark, scoring 21.1% accuracy compared to OpenAI's 26.6%, and significantly outperforming DeepSeek R1 (8.6%) and Gemini (7.2%).

The feature represents a shift in how users interact with AI chatbots: rather than iterating through multiple prompts, users can submit a single detailed query and receive a comprehensive multi-page report, downloadable as a PDF. Perplexity Pro subscribers ($20/month) get up to 500 Deep Research queries per month, compared to OpenAI's 120 queries for Pro users.

## Main Ideas

- Perplexity AI is the first major provider to offer a Deep Research feature for free, with five queries per day for free users and 500/month for Pro subscribers.
- Deep Research produces comprehensive, white-paper-style reports from a single prompt, shifting interaction away from iterative multi-prompt conversations.
- Perplexity's version is faster (2-4 minutes) but shallower than OpenAI's (up to 20 minutes), trading depth for speed and accessibility.
- On Humanity's Last Exam, Perplexity Deep Research scored 21.1% — close to OpenAI's 26.6% and well ahead of DeepSeek R1 and Gemini.
- The pricing gap is significant: Perplexity offers free access while OpenAI restricts Deep Research to paid tiers, with the best experience at $200/month.
- More specific and verbose prompts produce better Deep Research results, and completed reports can be exported as PDFs.
