# Exclusive: Anthropic Drops Flagship Safety Pledge

**Original source:** [Exclusive: Anthropic Drops Flagship Safety Pledge](https://time.com/7380854/exclusive-anthropic-drops-flagship-safety-pledge/)
**Author:** Billy Perrigo

## Summary

Anthropic dropped the central pledge of its Responsible Scaling Policy (RSP)—the 2023 commitment to never train an AI system unless it could guarantee in advance that safety measures were adequate. Chief science officer Jared Kaplan told TIME that the company decided "it wouldn't actually help anyone for us to stop training AI models" while competitors blaze ahead. The new policy commits to matching or surpassing competitors' safety efforts and to "delaying" development only if Anthropic considers itself the AI race leader and the catastrophic risks significant—a far less constraining framework than the previous categorical bar on training above certain capability levels.

The change reflects several converging pressures. The hoped-for regulations never materialized—the Trump Administration endorsed a "let-it-rip" attitude, no federal AI law is on the horizon, and the window for global governance has closed. The science of AI evaluations proved more complicated than expected: Anthropic couldn't definitively rule out models facilitating bio-terrorism but also lacked strong evidence they did, making the supposed "bright red line" look more like a "fuzzy gradient." Meanwhile, Anthropic's commercial success accelerated—a $30 billion raise at $380 billion valuation, revenue growing 10x per year, and Claude Code winning devoted fans.

METR's policy director Chris Painter called the change an understandable but "bearish signal," saying it shows Anthropic "believes it needs to shift into triage mode with its safety plans, because methods to assess and mitigate risk are not keeping up with the pace of capabilities." The new policy includes "Frontier Safety Roadmaps" with detailed future safety goals and "Risk Reports" published every 3-6 months. Painter expressed concern that moving away from binary capability tripwires might enable a "frog-boiling" effect where danger ramps up gradually without triggering alarms.

## Main Ideas

- Anthropic scrapped its 2023 pledge to never train models without guaranteed adequate safety measures
- The new policy only commits to delaying development if Anthropic is both the race leader and sees significant catastrophic risk
- Hoped-for regulations never materialized: no federal AI law, Trump favors deregulation, global governance window closed
- AI safety evaluations proved harder than expected—"bright red lines" turned out to be "fuzzy gradients"
- Commercial success (10x revenue growth, $380B valuation) coincided with the policy shift
- METR called it evidence that "society is not prepared for the potential catastrophic risks posed by AI"
- New commitments include Frontier Safety Roadmaps and Risk Reports every 3-6 months, but lack the previous binary tripwires

## Key Quotes

- "We felt that it wouldn't actually help anyone for us to stop training AI models. We didn't really feel, with the rapid advance of AI, that it made sense for us to make unilateral commitments … if competitors are blazing ahead." — Jared Kaplan, Anthropic CSO
- "This is more evidence that society is not prepared for the potential catastrophic risks posed by AI." — Chris Painter, METR
