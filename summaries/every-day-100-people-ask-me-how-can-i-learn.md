# Every day, 100+ people ask me, "How can I learn AI evals?"

**Original source:** [Every day, 100+ people ask me, "How can I learn..."](https://x.com/pauliusztin_/status/2046567126597861566/?s=12&t=lja9AMD11WXSlUvoMkbJMw&rw_tt_thread=True)
**Author:** Paul Iusztin

## Summary

Paul Iusztin shares a curated collection of 11 essential resources for learning AI evaluation (evals), a critical but often overlooked aspect of AI development. He reports receiving 100+ daily inquiries about this topic and has compiled this list to provide comprehensive guidance to learners. The resources span multiple dimensions of evaluation including observability, LLM-as-a-judge techniques, evaluation-driven development, RAG-specific evals, metric design, error analysis, and the limitations of automated LLM evaluators. This collection addresses both foundational concepts and practical implementation considerations, offering learners a structured pathway from understanding evaluation basics to mastering nuanced evaluation methodologies and recognizing common pitfalls.

## Main Ideas

- **LLM-as-a-judge is a key technique** but requires careful implementation and understanding of its limitations
- **Six core evaluation types exist for RAG systems**, suggesting evaluation needs can be systematized
- **Generic AI metrics are often misleading**—evaluation approaches must be tailored to specific use cases
- **Evaluation-driven development** is a systematic approach that should inform AI system design
- **Error analysis is fundamental** to iterating and improving AI systems effectively
- **Binary vs. Likert scale evaluations** represent different methodological choices with distinct tradeoffs
- **Observability and evaluation** are interconnected aspects of AI system monitoring and improvement
