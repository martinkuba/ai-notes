# Salesforce's new AI platform lets companies build their own digital agents to automate tasks

**Original source:** [Salesforce's new AI platform lets companies build their own digital agents to automate tasks](https://www.businessinsider.com/salesforce-agentforce-ai-tool-automate-tasks-boost-productivity-2024-11)
**Author:** Matthew S. Smith

## Summary

Salesforce has launched Agentforce, a platform enabling its 150,000+ enterprise clients to build and deploy AI agents that automate tasks such as generating reports from sales data and summarizing Slack conversations. Agentforce represents the latest evolution of Salesforce's AI capabilities, progressing from the original Einstein (2016), to Einstein GPT, to Einstein Copilot, and now to Agentforce with its prebuilt and customizable agents.

The platform is powered by the proprietary Atlas Reasoning Engine, which uses ReAct (Reasoning + Acting) prompting to break down problems into steps, evaluate results after each action, and iterate until reaching a satisfactory outcome. While Atlas uses Salesforce's own LLMs, it also integrates third-party models from OpenAI, Anthropic, Amazon, and Google. Salesforce addresses data privacy concerns with third-party models through a no-retention policy and safeguards against inappropriate responses. The platform includes Agentbuilder for creating custom agents that respond to triggers like incoming emails, and uses retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) to answer questions from company internal documents. Early results show 90% of customer inquiries resolved by agents, and Salesforce aims to build a large ecosystem of agent skills and topics.

## Main Ideas

- Agentforce evolved from Salesforce's earlier Einstein AI through multiple iterations, reflecting the shift toward generative AI and agentic systems
- The Atlas Reasoning Engine uses ReAct prompting for iterative problem-solving, similar in concept to OpenAI's chain-of-thought approach
- Agents are not monolithic single-LLM systems but can integrate multiple models from different providers
- Agentbuilder enables custom agents that act on triggers (emails, sales events) without direct human prompting
- RAG integration allows agents to answer questions from private company documents and data
- Slack integration surfaces AI agents where employees already spend their time
