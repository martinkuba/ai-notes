# The Modern AI PM in the age of Agents

**Original source:** [The Modern AI PM in the age of Agents](https://x.com/saboo_shubham_/status/2008742211194913117/?s=12&t=lja9AMD11WXSlUvoMkbJMw&rw_tt_thread=True)
**Author:** Shubham Saboo

## Summary

Shubham Saboo, a PM at Google, argues that the product manager role is fundamentally transforming as AI agents compress the "translation layer" between customer needs and shipped product. Previously, PMs translated customer problems into specs for engineers through a multi-week cycle of handoff, questions, implementation, and review. Now, PMs write clear problem statements, point agents at them, and review working code in an hour. The bottleneck has shifted from implementation capacity to knowing what's actually worth building.

Saboo identifies three critical new PM skills. First, "problem shaping" -- taking ambiguous customer pain and forming it into something precise enough for agents to act on, with clear constraints and success criteria. Second, "context curation" -- maintaining rich context documents about specific users, their exact words, examples of "good," failed approaches, shaping constraints, and measurable success criteria. He learned that vague prompts produce technically correct but pointless outputs, while rich context produces fitting solutions. Third, "evaluation and taste" -- the ability to judge whether agent output actually solves the problem or just technically works. The new workflow shifts from PM-writes-spec-engineers-build to PM-prototypes-with-agents-then-hands-to-engineers-for-production. Engineers become collaborators on making products production-ready rather than translators of PM intent. Saboo advises PMs to hold ambiguity longer, build multiple approaches in parallel, and develop intuition through repetition with real problems.

## Main Ideas

- The PM's "translation layer" between customers and engineers is compressing as agents can turn well-formed problems into working code
- The spec is becoming the product: problem definition and prototype are merging into one step
- Three critical new skills: problem shaping, context curation, and evaluation/taste
- Context curation -- feeding agents specific user details, real quotes, examples of "good," and failed approaches -- is the key to quality output
- The new PM workflow: prototype with agents first, then hand working software to engineers for production-readiness
- If your PM role was primarily translating needs into documents, that workflow gets automated; deep problem understanding is what remains valuable

## Key Quotes

- "The spec is becoming the product."
- "If your job was mostly translating customer needs into documents for engineers, that's a workflow. Workflows get automated."
- "The PMs who will thrive are the ones who understand problems so clearly that the solution becomes obvious to them and to the agents they work with."
