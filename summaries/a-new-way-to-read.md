# A New Way to Read

**Original source:** [A New Way to Read](https://every.to/source-code/a-new-way-to-read)
**Author:** Naveen Naidu Mummana

## Summary

Naveen Naidu Mummana describes building Kairos, an AI-native reading companion app that integrates AI directly into the book-reading experience. The idea emerged from the author's habit of toggling between an ebook reader and ChatGPT to ask questions about passages. Rather than treating AI as a separate tool, Kairos embeds an AI companion within a familiar reading interface (similar to Apple Books), enabling readers to chat, get summaries, and explore ideas without leaving the text.

The article uses Mortimer Adler's four levels of reading from "How to Read a Book" (1940) as a framework to demonstrate how AI transforms each level. At the elementary level, Kairos translates dense philosophical texts like Marcus Aurelius' Meditations into relatable modern language. At the inspectional level, it provides chapter summaries and catch-up features for maintaining context across long reading sessions. For analytical reading, the AI poses thought-provoking questions that push readers beyond passive consumption into critical engagement. Most ambitiously, at the syntopical level, Kairos enables cross-text conversations -- for example, connecting a modern book on goal-setting with ancient Hindu philosophy from the Bhagavad Gita.

The author argues this represents a genuine paradigm shift: reading transforms from a solitary, one-directional activity into a dynamic conversation. Rather than making readers lazy, AI-assisted reading requires active engagement -- formulating questions, evaluating responses, and synthesizing understanding. The article draws a historical arc from the printing press democratizing access, to digital technology democratizing distribution, to AI potentially democratizing the deep engagement with texts that was previously available only to scholars with expert tutors.

## Main Ideas

- Kairos is an AI-native reading app that embeds an AI companion directly into the book-reading experience, rather than treating AI as a separate tool.
- Mortimer Adler's four reading levels (elementary, inspectional, analytical, syntopical) provide a useful framework for understanding how AI enhances reading at each stage.
- AI transforms reading from a solitary, passive activity into a dynamic, conversational one that encourages critical thinking.
- Syntopical reading -- connecting ideas across books, cultures, and time periods -- becomes dramatically more accessible with AI assistance.
- AI-assisted reading requires active engagement (formulating questions, evaluating answers), countering the concern that it makes readers lazy.

## Key Quotes

- "Something odd happens when you read with AI. You start having conversations with books."
- "Centuries ago, the printing press democratized access to books. Digital technology has democratized distribution. AI might finally democratize the kind of deep engagement with texts that scholars and philosophers have practiced for centuries."
