# OpenAI reveals Swarm — a breakthrough new method for getting AI to do things on your behalf

**Original source:** [OpenAI reveals Swarm — a breakthrough new method for getting AI to do things on your behalf | Tom's Guide](https://www.tomsguide.com/ai/chatgpt/openai-reveals-swarm-a-new-open-source-method-for-getting-ai-to-do-things-on-your-behalf)
**Author:** Nigel Powell

## Summary

OpenAI released the Swarm Framework, an open-source project that provides developers with tools for building multi-agent AI systems capable of cooperating autonomously to complete tasks. While OpenAI characterized it as a research and educational experiment, the article argues it signals a broader shift in the AI industry from simple chatbot interactions toward agentic AI systems that can perform tasks and achieve goals over extended periods without continuous prompting.

The article traces the evolution of AI from general-purpose LLMs to fine-tuned domain-specific models, and now to "System 2" AI that incorporates reasoning capabilities (as seen in OpenAI's o1 model) and autonomous agency. Companies like Factory.ai already deploy software engineering agents that automate enterprise application development, and Sierra AI operates a customer support bot paid per successful interaction rather than via subscription — exemplifying Sequoia Capital's concept of "Outcome as a Service" replacing traditional SaaS models.

The piece draws on perspectives from Dario Amodei (Anthropic CEO), who envisions AI agents that can be given tasks taking hours, days, or weeks and execute them autonomously, and from AI pioneer Ilya Sutskever, who spoke of a "society of agents" as early as 2018. While acknowledging the transformative potential, the article tempers expectations by noting physical constraints, inherently unpredictable systems, and irreducible minimums in project timelines that even powerful AI cannot overcome.

## Main Ideas

- OpenAI's Swarm Framework enables the creation of multi-agent AI systems that cooperate autonomously to complete complex tasks, moving beyond single-prompt chatbot interactions.
- The AI industry is shifting from general-purpose LLMs to specialized, reasoning-capable "System 2" models that combine longer thinking time with agentic capabilities.
- Agentic AI is driving a new business model shift from Software as a Service (SaaS) to "Outcome as a Service" (OaaS), where AI is paid for completed tasks rather than subscription access.
- Companies like Factory.ai and Sierra AI are already deploying production agent systems in software engineering and customer support.
- Dario Amodei envisions millions of agents collaborating as a "country of geniuses in a datacenter," while acknowledging real-world constraints on what even powerful AI can achieve.
- The convergence of reasoning, agency, and advanced voice interfaces points toward deeply integrated AI assistants in daily life, though the exact timeline remains uncertain.
