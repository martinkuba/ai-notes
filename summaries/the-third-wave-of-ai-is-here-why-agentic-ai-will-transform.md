# The Third Wave Of AI Is Here: Why Agentic AI Will Transform The Way We Work

**Original source:** [The Third Wave Of AI Is Here: Why Agentic AI Will Transform The Way We Work](https://www.forbes.com/sites/bernardmarr/2024/11/15/the-third-wave-of-ai-is-here-why-agentic-ai-will-transform-the-way-we-work/)
**Author:** Bernard Marr

## Summary

This article outlines the three waves of artificial intelligence and argues that we are now entering the third and most transformative wave: agentic AI. The first wave was predictive AI, which enabled businesses to forecast trends and make data-driven decisions. The second wave brought generative AI, allowing machines to create content and hold conversations. Now, agentic AI introduces autonomous agents that can independently perform complex tasks, make decisions within defined parameters, and interact with other AI agents — all without constant human oversight.

Silvio Savarese, EVP and Chief Scientist of Salesforce AI Research, envisions a future where specialized AI agents handle roles from customer service to sales development, with humans serving as orchestrators who coordinate and manage teams of AI agents. Practical applications are already emerging: AI agents that handle support tickets end-to-end, negotiate deals with other agents, create patient summaries in healthcare, and participate actively in meetings. New job categories like AI agent trainers, workflow orchestrators, and ethics compliance officers are beginning to appear.

The article emphasizes that deploying agentic AI comes with significant responsibilities, including the need for sophisticated guardrails and a "human at the helm" principle to maintain control over critical decision points. Organizations are urged to explore agentic AI now, as success in this new paradigm requires professionals to understand AI capabilities and limitations, delegate effectively to agents, and develop new competencies in orchestrating human-AI collaboration.

## Main Ideas

- AI has progressed through three waves: predictive, generative, and now agentic AI
- Agentic AI agents can autonomously execute tasks, make decisions, and interact with other agents
- Humans will shift to orchestrator roles, managing teams of specialized AI agents
- New job categories are emerging around AI agent management and governance
- Trust and control remain critical — guardrails and "human at the helm" principles are essential
- Organizations should begin exploring agentic AI now to stay competitive
