# Create AI-Powered Apps with Claude Artifacts - No Coding Required

**Original source:** [Create AI-Powered Apps with Claude Artifacts - No Coding Required](https://www.anthropic.com/news/build-artifacts?ref=aisecret.us)
**Author:** anthropic.com

## Summary

Anthropic announced a major update to Claude's artifacts feature: a dedicated artifacts space in the Claude app and the ability to embed AI capabilities directly into creations, transforming artifacts into interactive, AI-powered apps. Since launch, millions of users have created over half a billion artifacts—from productivity tools to educational games. The update lets anyone create even more interactive experiences without coding, such as a flashcard app that generates cards on any user-chosen topic (rather than a static set) or a personalized storytelling app.

The new artifacts space offers browsing curated artifacts for inspiration, customizing existing creations, building from scratch through conversation, and organizing everything in one place. Music producer Rick Rubin explored this concept with "The Way of Code," pairing 81 meditations with interactive artifacts that anyone can reshape. The feature is available to Free, Pro, and Max users, with AI-powered artifact capabilities in beta. Artifacts can be shared publicly, though viewers need to sign in to get the full interactive experience.

## Main Ideas

- Claude artifacts can now embed AI capabilities, turning them into interactive apps rather than static creations

- No coding is required—users describe ideas in conversation and Claude builds shareable apps, tools, and games
- The dedicated artifacts space enables browsing, customizing, building, and organizing creations
