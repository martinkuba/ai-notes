# The Present Future: AI's Impact Long Before Superintelligence

**Original source:** [The Present Future: AI's Impact Long Before Superintelligence](https://www.oneusefulthing.org/p/the-present-future-ais-impact-long?utm_medium=email)
**Author:** Ethan Mollick

## Summary

Ethan Mollick argues that even without superintelligent AI, today's Gen2/GPT-4 class systems already possess more capability than society has absorbed, and would drive years of transformation even if AI development stopped entirely. Rather than debating speculative superintelligence timelines, Mollick demonstrates through practical examples what current multimodal AI can already do when combined with tool use and computer vision.

His first demonstration uses Claude to monitor a construction site video, analyzing worker safety equipment, material placement, and potential hazards, then automatically generating a prioritized punch list spreadsheet and tracking system. While acknowledging likely hallucinations, he argues such AI monitoring could be valuable where no human oversight currently exists. His second example has Claude act as a naive user testing Walmart and Amazon websites, producing solid usability reports with no hallucinations detected. A third example shows an AI-powered avatar joining a Zoom call, completely AI-generated from voice to image to behavior. Mollick frames these capabilities as raising urgent policy questions: AI monitoring could be a mentor and safety check or could become a panopticon; AI assistants could augment or gradually replace human judgment. The critical choices about deployment are being made now, and he warns that organizations must move beyond viewing AI as a purely technical challenge to consider human impact, ensuring these tools enhance rather than diminish human potential and agency.

## Main Ideas

- Current AI capabilities already exceed what society has integrated, making superintelligence debates less urgent than present-day deployment questions
- Multimodal AI + tool use enables practical applications like automated safety monitoring, website testing, and virtual meeting avatars
- AI monitoring can be a mentor/safety tool or a panopticon -- the choice depends on organizational and policy decisions being made now
- AI is already a "reasonable intern" that executes assignments quickly using judgment, with quality improving without new models
- AI-powered virtual avatars are crossing the uncanny valley and may soon fool people in video calls
- The transformation of work is happening through current-generation AI, not some future breakthrough

## Key Quotes

- "Even if AI development stopped today, we would have years of change ahead of us integrating these systems into our world."
- "The choices companies make, and the rules put in place by governments, will determine whether AI is used to help or to monitor us."
