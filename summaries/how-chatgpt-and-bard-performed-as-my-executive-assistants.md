# How ChatGPT and Bard Performed as My Executive Assistants

**Original source:** [How ChatGPT and Bard Performed as My Executive Assistants](https://www.nytimes.com/2023/03/29/technology/personaltech/ai-chatgpt-google-bard-assistant.html)
**Author:** Brian X. Chen

## Summary

Brian X. Chen tested ChatGPT and Google's Bard as executive assistants across four common tasks: meeting preparation, summarizing meetings, trip planning, and calendar management. For each task, both chatbots were told to assume the role of executive assistant to the CEO of a fictional AI startup. The experiment revealed a stark performance gap between the two systems as of early 2023.

ChatGPT performed well across most tasks. For meeting preparation, it produced a detailed background check on former Apple executive Scott Forstall and generated tailored talking points, including referencing his academic background in Symbolic Systems and his ethical concerns about AI. Forstall himself called ChatGPT's recommendations "startlingly bespoke and cogent." For trip planning, ChatGPT created a thoughtful Taipei itinerary with specific hotel and restaurant recommendations and built in jet lag recovery time. Its meeting summaries were detailed with clear action plans. Bard, by contrast, produced generic, vague outputs across all tasks -- offering unspecific talking points, failing to recommend hotels or restaurants, ignoring weekend leisure requests, and misassigning action items in meeting summaries. Google explained that Bard's cautious approach to personal information was intentional and that the product was still an early experiment.

Neither chatbot could manage calendars since they lacked access to external services, though both companies signaled this capability was coming. The broader implication Chen drew was that AI chatbots can already handle many administrative tasks well enough to potentially shift or replace human executive assistant roles, particularly for repetitious work -- a finding with significant implications for white-collar employment.

## Main Ideas

- ChatGPT significantly outperformed Google's Bard across all four executive assistant tasks tested in early 2023.
- ChatGPT produced specific, tailored outputs (meeting prep, travel itineraries, meeting summaries) while Bard's responses were generic and often missed key requirements.
- Neither chatbot could manage calendars due to lack of third-party service integration, though both companies were working toward this.
- The former Apple executive who was the subject of the meeting prep test called ChatGPT's output "startlingly bespoke and cogent" and Bard's "comically generic."
- AI chatbots could already handle enough administrative work to potentially shift or replace human executive assistant roles.
- Google defended Bard's cautious approach as intentional for an early-stage product, particularly regarding personal information.
