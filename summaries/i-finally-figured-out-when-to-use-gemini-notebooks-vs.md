# I finally figured out when to use Gemini Notebooks vs NotebookLM — here's the winning workflow

**Original source:** [I finally figured out when to use Gemini Notebooks vs NotebookLM — here's the winning workflow](https://www.tomsguide.com/ai/i-finally-figured-out-when-to-use-gemini-notebooks-vs-notebooklm-heres-the-winning-workflow)
**Author:** Amanda Caswell

## Summary

Google's two notebook-style AI tools—NotebookLM and Gemini Notebooks—serve distinctly different purposes despite their similar names and overlapping functionality. NotebookLM excels at grounded research and understanding complex information, making it ideal for deep dives into subjects where accuracy and source verification matter. It allows users to upload multiple document types (PDFs, Google Docs, websites, transcripts) and generates source-cited answers, summaries, Audio Overviews, study guides, and FAQs. Gemini Notebooks, by contrast, is optimized for the creative phase—transforming research insights into finished work like blog posts, presentations, emails, and marketing materials. It offers broader access to live information and tools, making it better suited for brainstorming, combining notes with current data, and finalizing shareable content. The real power emerges when both tools are used sequentially: use NotebookLM for research and understanding, then move to Gemini Notebooks for creation and refinement. This two-stage workflow eliminates the confusion of trying to force one tool to handle both research and creation, allowing each to excel at its intended purpose while creating a seamless pipeline from information gathering to polished output.

## Main Ideas

- **NotebookLM is optimized for research mode**: Upload multiple sources, ask grounded questions, generate accurate summaries with source citations, and create alternative formats like Audio Overviews and study guides
- **Gemini Notebooks is optimized for creation mode**: Transform rough research notes and ideas into polished, shareable outputs like articles, presentations, and marketing materials
- **Source grounding reduces AI drift**: NotebookLM's reliance on uploaded materials prevents the random tangents that can occur with standard AI chat interfaces
- **Sequential workflow beats forced multipurpose use**: Using NotebookLM first for research, then moving to Gemini Notebooks for creation, is more effective than trying to use one tool for both stages
- **Practical applications span multiple domains**: The workflow benefits professionals managing meeting notes and project documents, students studying course materials, and anyone organizing personal information
- **Tool integration preserves work history**: Keeping everything in Gemini Notebooks after the creation phase allows for easy reference, progress tracking, and future iteration
