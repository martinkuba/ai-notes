# Businesses must reinvent themselves in the age of agentic AI

**Original source:** [Businesses must reinvent themselves in the age of agentic AI](https://www.zdnet.com/article/businesses-must-reinvent-themselves-in-the-age-of-agentic-ai/)
**Author:** Vala Afshar

## Summary

Based on Accenture's global survey of 1,500 companies, this article outlines how businesses should build a "reinvention-ready digital core" to thrive in the age of agentic AI. Organizations following three key principles—Architect with intent, Connect the dots, and Thrive with ecosystems (ACT)—achieve 60% higher revenue growth with 40% higher margins. The report emphasizes a shift from traditional instruction-driven systems to intention-driven systems powered by AI that mimic human-like thinking, learning, and action.

"Architect with intent" means evolving to a cognitive architecture where AI serves as the central orchestrator, with leaders adopting an AI-first mindset that reinvents operations rather than retrofitting AI into existing processes. 76% of IT executives identify IT as the primary area for Gen AI transformation. "Connect the dots" focuses on seamlessly integrating systems of record with systems of insight to enable real-time analytics and hyper-personalized actions, requiring CIOs and CDOs to collaborate closely. "Thrive with ecosystems" encourages leveraging larger platform ecosystems (AWS, Google Cloud, Salesforce) to access early-stage innovations through marketplaces and partnerships.

The article positions agentic AI as the top strategic technology trend for 2025, noting that while one-third of consumers would prefer working with AI agents for faster service, only 11% of CIOs had fully implemented the technology, with security and data infrastructure as top impediments. The pressure to adopt is so strong that CIOs must effectively serve as chief AI officers, and companies that don't act risk being left behind as competitors advance.

## Main Ideas

- Organizations following the ACT principles (Architect, Connect, Thrive) achieve 60% higher revenue growth with 40% better margins
- Businesses must shift from instruction-driven to intention-driven systems with AI as the central orchestrator
- 76% of IT executives see IT as the primary area for Gen AI transformation in the next three years
- Seamless integration of transaction and analytical platforms is essential for real-time, AI-driven decision-making
- Only 11% of CIOs have fully implemented AI despite recognizing it as a game-changer
- Larger ecosystem partners now provide access to early-stage innovations, reducing the need to wait for commercial stability
